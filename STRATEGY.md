# Hola Translator — Sales Intelligence Strategy

## Core Concept
Door-to-door sales app for Texas. Targets Spanish-speaking customers.
Not just translation — **intent detection + sales coaching**.

## Wake Word: "Hola"
- English speakers never say "Hola" — perfect trigger word
- When detected: app activates, listens, translates in ear
- Rep hears coached response in English AND suggested Spanish reply

## Intent Tiers (Speed Priority)
| Layer | What | Speed |
|-------|------|-------|
| 1 | Keyword snap | <200ms |
| 2 | Phrase match | ~300ms |
| 3 | Spanglish hybrid match | ~400ms |
| 4 | Full translation fallback | ~800ms |

**Layer 3 (Spanglish) = secret weapon. No one else builds this.**

## Signal Categories

### 🟢 BUYING SIGNALS (CLOSE NOW)
- "Pues háganle."
- "Órale, sí."
- "¿Qué ocupo?" / "¿Qué necesitan de mí?"
- "¿Dónde firmo?"
- "¿Nada más es eso?"
- "Pues si no hay contrato, sí."
- "¿Cuándo empiezan?" / "¿Lo pueden hacer ahorita?"
- "Sí me interesa."
- "Pásele." (inviting you in)
- "Espéreme, deje traigo mi ID."
- "Suena bien." / "Está bien, vamos a probar."

### 🟡 HOT LEAD (Provider Frustration)

- "Ya estoy harto de Spectrum."
